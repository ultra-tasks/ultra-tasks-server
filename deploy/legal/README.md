# Operator documents

Before opening public registration, provide your own `privacy.html` here. `terms.html` is optional. These documents are served from the account origin at `/privacy.html` and `/terms.html`; they are not inherited upstream policies. Production requires acceptance before account creation and records its timestamp.

Configure the operator identity, contact and hosting/SMTP details for the actual deployment. This repository does not supply those statements on an operator's behalf. Keep enrollment closed until the documents and deployment have been reviewed.
