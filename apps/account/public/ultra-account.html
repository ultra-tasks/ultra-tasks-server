Save your recovery key

Your recovery key is the only way to get your encrypted tasks back if you forget your password and have no signed-in device. Ultra Tasks cannot recover it for you. Copy it into your password manager, save the file, or print it.

If you lose both your password and your recovery key and have no unlocked device, you will lose access to your encrypted data.

Each recovery code works once if you lose your authenticator or security key. Save them somewhere private now. They are shown only this once, and adding another method replaces this set.
